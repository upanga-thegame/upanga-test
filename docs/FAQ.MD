# Upanga FAQ

## Game

### What is Upanga: The Soul Blade?
Upanga: The Soul Blade is a tactical, turn-based fantasy RPG set in Bomende, a land whose kingdoms have been divided by the return of an ancient power.

### Who do I play as?
You command four heroes: Osa the Warrior, Aziza the Scout, Nganga the Shaman, and Kishi the Shapeshifter. Each hero brings a different way to approach an isometric battle.

### What stage of development is Upanga in?
Upanga is in active development. Core systems such as movement, turn order, combat, UI, and save/load are being built and tested while the story and cinematic scenes continue to take shape.

### Will Upanga be free?
The current plan is for the game to be free to download, with the first part available to play and additional Chapter 1 content available for a small price.

## Gameplay

### What kind of combat does Upanga use?
Upanga uses initiative-based, tactical turn-based combat on an isometric grid. Players command heroes, move across tiles, spend action points, and choose when to attack, defend, heal, or reposition.

### Is Upanga online or offline?
Upanga is planned as a single-player game. Online features are not required for the core experience.

### What is the Soul Blade?
The Soul Blade was forged by the kingdoms of Bomende as a symbol of their shared alliance. It shattered while imprisoning Bida, and its four pieces are now guarded across the Crypt, Swamp, Kwae, and Pyramid.

## Release

### When will Upanga be released?

### What platforms are planned?
The current release plan includes Android, Apple devices, and Steam.
